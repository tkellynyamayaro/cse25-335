About Stellar Cinemas

Gaborone’s premier cinema experience since 2010

Our Story

Stellar Cinemas was founded in 2010 with a mission to bring world-class cinema experiences to Botswana. Located at Molapo Crossing in Gaborone, we have grown into a multiplex serving movie lovers every week.

We showcase both local and international films while offering affordable prices and a comfortable environment for all ages.
